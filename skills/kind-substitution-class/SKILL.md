# Kind-Substitution Class

Dropping one published kind axis, with every other conversion held fixed,
manufactures a straddle of a named residual that the intact rectangle
does not contain.

## Kill

If the axis was not published, KSC scores 0.
If the intact corners already straddle, KSC scores 0.
If the stripped corners do not straddle, KSC scores 0.
If the stripped row received a spatial or unit promotion the intact row
did not, KSC scores 0.

## Do not re-issue

RSC, FSC, RGC, DPC, PDC, FPC, IRC, Column-Node Veto, the fish 500 m
rectangle, the land-sink ±1.1 GtC interval, dark oxygen.

## Leftover (do not mint here)

Kind-Overshoot: intact corners all on one side of the residual, and a
substituted figure all on the other side, without a straddle and without
being one rectangle. Visser 2025 is only an extract-level witness.
Mint later only if one source's deleted axis produces that sign flip.
